# Facet policy for reviewed gold-compatible extraction — 1

Use only the supplied title and abstract. Problem describes the limitation or
question, task the operational objective, method how it is performed, dataset a
named data resource used, and contribution a claimed new artifact or finding.
Concise faithful paraphrases and context-supported references are allowed.

Preserve uncertainty, negation, quantities and the scope of each claim. Independent
findings and limitations may be separate labels; do not drop a condition essential
to the meaning of the finding. Labels need not reproduce entire source sentences.

Automatic validation confirms schema and source attribution, rejects explicit
inconsistencies and records warnings for lexical/scope review. It cannot establish
semantic entailment, novelty, correct facet role or complete coverage. The source
review pass must check these. Generated labels remain unreviewed until reviewed.
